My Routine

Two-week training cycle
Day 1 – Squat Day · Lower body strength

Warm-Up (8–10 min)

  • 3–5 min incline walk or bike
  • Hip circles ×10/side
  • Bodyweight squats ×15
  • Glute bridges ×15
  • Ankle rocks ×10/side

Main Lifts

  • Back Squat — 4×6 — 215–235 lbs
  • Bulgarian Split Squat — 3×10/leg — 35 lbs/side
  • Romanian Deadlift (RDL) — 2×10 — 215–235 lbs
  • Standing Calf Raises — 3×15

Core

  • Plank Variations — 3×40 sec

Shoulder Health

  • Band External Rotations — 2×15

Mobility

  • Couch Stretch — 1 min/side
  • Seated Hamstring Stretch — 1 min/side
  • Pigeon Stretch — 90 sec/side
  • Calf Wall Stretch — 45 sec/side
  • Deep Breathing Reset — 2 min
Day 2 – Bench Press Day · Upper push

Warm-Up

  • Arm circles ×15
  • Band pull-aparts ×20
  • Scapular push-ups ×12
  • Light shoulder mobility

Main Lifts

  • Bench Press — 4×6 — 195 lbs
  • Overhead Press — 3×8 — 50 lbs
  • Incline Dumbbell Press — 3×10 — 45–50 lbs
  • Lateral Raises — 3×12 — 25 lbs
  • Triceps Dips — 3×12

Shoulder Stability

  • Scapular Wall Slides — 2×12

Mobility

  • Doorway Pec Stretch — 45 sec/side
  • Child’s Pose with Side Reach — 45 sec/side
  • Thoracic Open Books — 10 reps/side
  • Neck Stretch — 30 sec/side
  • Deep Breathing Reset — 2 min
